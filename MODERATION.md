# Moderation policy

- Every module enters through a pull request.
- Declarative modules require automated validation and one maintainer approval.
- WASM modules additionally require permission, source, and release reproducibility review.
- Review labels are `reviewed`, `limited-review`, and `blocked`; none is a security guarantee.
- Malware, credential theft, undeclared behavior, impersonation, abandoned critical vulnerabilities, and valid legal takedowns can cause immediate delisting or revocation.
- Maintainers do not promise a review deadline. Rejected submissions receive a reason and may resubmit.
